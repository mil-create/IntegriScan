# IntegriScan - Your Skin & Scalp Health Companion

## What Is This App?
IntegriScan is a simple, friendly health assistant that helps you monitor your skin and scalp health using just your smartphone. Think of it as your personal skin health journal that lives in your pocket - it helps you check, track, and understand changes in your skin over time.

**Who is this for?**
- Anyone who wants to keep an eye on moles, spots, or skin changes
- People concerned about scalp health or hair thinning
- Those managing skin conditions like eczema, psoriasis, or acne
- Anyone who wants to be proactive about their skin health
- Perfect for bringing organized information to doctor's appointments

## How To Get The Most Out Of IntegriScan

### For Best Results:
1. **Be Regular** - Try to check the same areas monthly (like the first Sunday of each month) to spot changes early
2. **Good Lighting** - When taking photos, use natural light from a window or take pictures outside in the shade
3. **Be Specific** - Instead of just "red spot," note: "red spot on left cheek, about the size of a pencil eraser, slightly raised"
4. **Track Changes** - If something looks different from last time, make sure to mention it in your symptoms
5. **Use the Photos** - Comparing photos side-by-side is often the best way to see subtle changes
6. **Share With Professionals** - Bring your Clinical Logs to dermatologist or doctor appointments

### When To Seek Professional Help:
While IntegriScan is a helpful monitoring tool, please see a healthcare professional if you notice:
- A mole or spot that changes in size, shape, or color
- A sore that doesn't heal within 2-3 weeks
- Persistent itching, pain, or bleeding from a skin area
- Rapidly growing bumps or lesions
- Any skin change that worries you - when in doubt, get it checked
